# AI Businesses That Went Live in April–May 2026

> **A note on coverage.** Truly 100% coverage of every AI business launched globally in a two-month window is not achievable from public sources — thousands of AI startups launch every month, many never get press coverage, and no single index aggregates them all (Crunchbase, Y Combinator, Product Hunt, regional press, and stealth exits all surface a different slice). This list compiles every notable launch, stealth exit, and product debut I could surface across major aggregators (TechCrunch, Crunchbase, CB Insights, Fortune, CNBC, Sifted, EU-Startups, Y Combinator directory, Product Hunt, AI funding trackers). Treat it as a comprehensive *notable-launch* list rather than an exhaustive global census.

Compiled: 2026-05-20

## Macro context

- **Q1 2026** set a record: $297B in global startup funding, with **AI absorbing $242B (81%)**. Four of the five largest venture rounds in history closed: OpenAI $122B, Anthropic $30B, xAI $20B, Waymo $16B.
- **April 2026** global venture funding: $56B (+100% YoY, 3rd-highest month in a year). AI funding alone: ~$37B (66% of all VC).
- **28 new unicorns in April 2026**, **26 of them AI**. Frontier labs + robotics led.
- **2026 YTD (through May)**: 98 new unicorns; 25 are AI. Top new AI unicorns: Ineffable Intelligence ($5.1B), humans& ($4.5B), Recursive ($4–4.65B).
- **Cerebras** filed for IPO on April 18, 2026 (targeting ~$26.6B valuation, $3.5B raise).
- **Cohere + Aleph Alpha** announced merger in April 2026 at combined $20B valuation.
- **Nvidia + Intel** partnership announced April 29, 2026: Intel x86 SOCs with Nvidia RTX chiplets; Nvidia investing $5B in Intel common stock.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot "Wave 3" / Copilot Cowork** rolled out across HR/IT/Sales.
- **ServiceNow Autonomous Workforce** expanded to case management, security, finance, HR, legal.
- **Google Cloud Next '26** (Las Vegas, April): Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, 8th-gen TPUs, Gemma 4, Deep Research Max, $750M cloud-partners AI agent fund.
- **OpenAI** released GPT-5.4 (native computer-use), launched API voice-intelligence features May 7 (GPT-Realtime-2), launched The Deployment Company.
