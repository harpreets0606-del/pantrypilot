# AI Tourism in Aotearoa New Zealand — Opportunity Map 2026

A scouting report on AI-based tourism products that exist globally, where NZ is already covered, and where there is real whitespace to build, license or resell a profitable business.

Prepared: May 2026. NZD figures use approximate FX of 1 USD = 1.65 NZD; 1 EUR = 1.80 NZD.

---

## 1. The bottom line (read this first)

NZ tourism is back to ~90% of pre-COVID volume (3.51M arrivals in 2025) and **NZD 46.6B total expenditure**, with international spend at **NZD 18.1B** (+7% YoY). The market wants AI tools — **75% of operators are experimenting, only ~33% report results**, and operators run an average of five disconnected systems. That mismatch is where money is made.

The **top 5 buildable plays for an NZ operator-entrepreneur**, ranked by attractiveness (market gap × buyer willingness × defensibility):

| # | Play | Target buyer | Indicative monthly $ (NZD) | Why it wins |
|---|------|--------------|---------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | **AI ops layer for tour operators** on top of Rezdy / Bókun — weather rebooking, dispatch, guide certification | ~1,500 adventure/tour operators | $100-$400 per location | Rezdy and Bókun own the bookings but are NOT AI-native; nobody serves weather-cancellation rebooking, which is the #1 NZ pain point |
| 2 | **VoiceMap-for-Aotearoa** — creator-marketplace audio walks/drives with iwi royalty share under a Kaitiakitanga-style license | Tourists ($), iwi/RTOs/DOC ($$) | $5-$20 per tour B2C; B2B licensing | Te Hiku's te-reo speech AI is world-leading; no NZ-native equivalent exists with proper iwi IP rights; benchmark prices already proven (Kruse charges NZ$99/45 days) |
| 3 | **AI carbon ingestion for Toitū certification** — auto-pull Genesis/Mercury/Z/RMS-Cloud data into a Toitū-ready GHG profile | ~1,000+ operators chasing Tiaki / Glasgow Declaration | $300-$1,500/mo | Toitū owns the audit authority but the workflow is manual; operators currently lose 40-100 hours/year to admin |
| 4 | **Mid-tier destination analytics for Regional Tourism Organisations** — Mabrian-style sentiment + flight demand + flow at an RTO price point | 31 RTOs (Christchurch, Queenstown, Auckland, Northland…) | NZD 25-60k/year per RTO | TNZ has GuideGeek; Mabrian/ADARA price for big national DMOs; the 31 RTOs have nothing tourism-specific |
| 5 | **Vertical AI concierge / hotel chatbot tuned for the NZ motel-and-lodge mid-market** — Mandarin/Korean/Japanese, RMS Cloud + Seekom integration | ~2,300 motels/holiday parks | $200-$600/mo per property | Canary/HiJiffy/Duve are global but have weak NZ presence; Yonder HQ (acquired by US TOMIS) and Myma.ai cover the top end only |

The categories to **avoid** entering as a new NZ player: general AI itinerary planners (Booking.com, Layla, GuideGeek already serve NZ travellers), generic hotel revenue-management systems (RoomPriceGenie, Atomize, Duetto, IDeaS have the market), generic rostering (Deputy and Tanda already won), and payments fraud (NZ tourism volume too small for Forter/Riskified).

## 2. NZ market context — what you'd be selling into

### Arrivals, spend, source markets
- **3.51M arrivals YE Dec 2025** (~90% of 2019). Source: Stats NZ International Travel.
- **NZD 46.6B total tourism expenditure** (YE Mar 2025); **NZD 18.1B international** (+7%). Source: MBIE Tourism Satellite Account.
- **Tourism = 6.8% of total employment.**
- Top markets: Australia 1.52M arrivals / NZD 3.6B spend · USA 385k / NZD 1.8B · China 262k / NZD 1.2B · UK 192k.
- **Seasonality is brutal:** Jan alone = ~20% of holiday nights; Jun = <5%. This is the structural pain that creates demand for AI workforce/forecasting tools.

### Who actually has budget
- **31 Regional Tourism Organisations** (RTNZ) — council-funded, $1-15M annual budgets.
- **60+ i-SITEs**, 350+ staff — captive walk-in distribution.
- **DOC Great Walks** — ~100k bookings/yr (35% international), NZD 9M+ from opening-day Great Walks 2025/26 alone; just rebuilt the booking system after Milford peak hit ~12,000 simultaneous waiters.
- **TIA membership = ~85% of tourism turnover** — single-door industry access.
- **Big operators with seven-figure tech budgets:** Skyline Enterprises (NZD 257M revenue, NZD 61M pre-tax profit FY25); Ngāi Tahu Tourism (iwi-owned, Shotover Jet / Dart River / Franz Josef Glacier); Real NZ (Milford cruises, Cardrona); AJ Hackett; THL motorhomes (NZX-listed); Air NZ (already on OpenAI Enterprise — 1,500+ Custom GPTs).
- **TOP 10 Holiday Parks** — 49 parks, 40-year co-operative, group-buying decision-maker.

### Government funding tailwinds
- **International Visitor Levy** raised to **NZD 100/visitor** (from $35) in late 2024. Ring-fenced ~**NZD 35M/yr new investment** (80/20 demand/supply initially). **Warning:** Treasury reportedly froze new IVL spending in Dec 2025 (Newsroom) — pipeline timing matters.
- **Sept 2025 "Major Events and Tourism" package: NZD 70M** (NZD 40M events attraction, NZD 10M boost, NZD 10M regional, NZD 10M infrastructure).
- **DOC: NZD 30M IVL** allocated specifically for conservation tourism projects.
- **Tourism Growth Roadmap target:** double tourism exports from NZD 9.9B (2023) to **NZD 19.8B by 2034**.
- **Callaghan Innovation is being disestablished** — R&D grants moved to MBIE "Innovation Services" from 1 Dec 2024. **RDTI tax credit (15%) still active.**
- **TINZT grants:** up to NZD 100k industry-wide projects, NZD 10k private enterprise.

### The buyer-pain shortlist (verbatim from operator commentary)
1. **Labour shortage / seasonal staffing** — projected 40% national shortage; tourism tertiary enrolments fell 63% (3,750 → 1,355 EFTS, 2015→2024).
2. **Multilingual frontline** — Chinese FITs replaced ADS group travel; in-language staff lost during COVID.
3. **Overcrowding at hotspots** — Queenstown alone took 7,794+ overnight freedom campers in 2024; QLDC Bylaw 2025 effective 1 Dec adds 15 restricted zones with NZD 400-800 fines.
4. **Weather-dependent rebooking** — Milford flights, glacier landings, jet boats — all handled by hand today.
5. **Carbon reporting against Tiaki Promise + Glasgow Declaration** — TIA signed May 2025; 98% of operators say sustainability matters, ~50% want online tools.
6. **EV road-trip planning** — gov target 10,000 chargers by 2030; EVRoam coverage gaps on South Island.
7. **Iwi cultural storytelling at scale** — demand exceeds supply; concentrated in Rotorua, Waitangi, Christchurch.

### Existing local tech ecosystem (don't compete with these without a reason)
- **Cobber** (the former ResBook merged with ResPax + Livn under Tomahawk) — NZ's biggest accommodation/tour PMS consolidation event.
- **Tourplan** (Auckland HQ, 400+ tour operators in 70 countries) — established DMC software.
- **Wherewolf** (NZ) — digital waivers / guest ops for adventure operators; used by AJ Hackett, skydive ops.
- **AskNicely** (Auckland) — NPS / frontline CX; ~USD 32M Series B (2022); 1,300+ customers; competes with Medallia rather than acquired by them.
- **Yonder HQ** (NZ-built hotel chatbot; powers Bolton Hotel "Bea", Cardrona "Pengi", Antarctic Centre) — **acquired by US agency TOMIS in 2024**.
- **Myma.ai / Book Me Bob** (Auckland) — hotel chatbot in 20 countries; Microsoft-mentored.
- **Hey Kiwi** (Queenstown) — pre-launch AI tour guide founded by Matt Wong (iFly) + Trent Yeo (Ziptrek); seeking ~NZD 13M.
- **Te Hiku Media** (Kaitaia) — te reo speech-to-text at 92% accuracy on NVIDIA NeMo; invented the **Kaitiakitanga License**, the cleanest indigenous data-governance framework globally.
- **Lightning Lab Tourism** (Christchurch Airport / Creative HQ) — 3-month accelerator; alumni include Amio (itinerary AI), S360 (sustainability), Stay Native (indigenous experiences).

### Cultural and regulatory constraints (these are not optional)
- **Te Tiriti o Waitangi obligations** — applies to any Crown-funded buyer (TNZ, DOC, MBIE, councils, RTOs).
- **Māori data sovereignty (Te Mana Raraunga)** — Rangatiratanga, Whakapapa, Whanaungatanga, Kotahitanga. Any AI trained on Māori place names, pūrākau or tikanga without iwi consent is high-risk.
- **OPC AI Guidance (Sep 2023)** — eight requirements for generative AI use including senior sign-off, Privacy Impact Assessment, **explicit engagement with Māori**, no model retention of personal data.
- **Privacy Act 2020 IPP 12** — cross-border data transfer rules apply (tourist from US, booking from UK, processed in NZ).
- **MBIE Responsible AI Guidance (2025)** — soft-law for now; no EU-AI-Act equivalent yet.

For RTO / DOC / council procurement, demonstrating iwi co-design BEFORE selling is effectively a gate. This is also a moat — overseas competitors can't fake it.

## 4. The five plays in detail (with rough revenue math)

### Play 1 — AI ops layer for NZ tour operators on top of Rezdy / Bókun

**Problem:** Adventure operators cancel daily for weather. Today a Milford cruise / glacier heli / jet-boat operator with 200 customers cancels via phone calls, SMS blasts, and manual Rezdy re-bookings. Guide certifications expire silently. Multi-vehicle dispatch is on whiteboards.

**Product:** A Rezdy/Bókun-overlay SaaS that:
1. Watches MetService forecasts + DOC alerts + GeoNet feeds.
2. Auto-proposes rebooking offers to affected customers (next available slot, refund, voucher) via SMS + WhatsApp + email in their language.
3. Tracks guide certifications (Maritime NZ MOSS, DOC concessions, heli-pilot hours, swift-water, first aid) with expiry alerts.
4. Optimises multi-vehicle dispatch and guide-to-customer matching.

**Buyers:** ~1,500 NZ adventure operators + a meaningful slice of ~3,000 Australian counterparts. Hit list:
- Tier 1 (NZD 400-1,000/mo): AJ Hackett, Real NZ, Skyline, Ngāi Tahu Tourism, Glacier Country Heli, Helipro, Wayfare.
- Tier 2 (NZD 200-400/mo): regional jet-boat, kayak, dolphin-swim, ski-touring operators.
- Tier 3 (NZD 100-200/mo): owner-operator guides.

**Math:** Capture 300 operators @ NZD 250/mo avg = NZD 900k ARR in year 1; 800 @ NZD 300 = NZD 2.9M ARR by year 3. Atomize-style multiple (8-12× ARR) → NZD 23-35M valuation by year 3 if executed well.

**Why you win:** Rezdy/Bókun cannot localise this fast — NZ weather is too quirky and their roadmap is global. Wherewolf (waivers) and Tourplan (DMC) are obvious distribution partners. AskNicely-style frontline-NPS founders are the right archetype.

**Risks:** Stonehenge (Rezdy owner) could buy you or close the API. Mitigation: ship multi-platform (Rezdy + Bókun + FareHarbor + Checkfront) and bake yourself into operator workflows.

### Play 2 — VoiceMap-for-Aotearoa with iwi royalty share

**Problem:** GuideAlong has NZ routes; Kruse charges NZ$99/45 days; nothing exists with proper iwi-permissioned Māori narration at scale and a creator royalty share. Te Hiku has the world's best te reo speech AI; nobody is using it commercially in tourism.

**Product:** A VoiceMap-clone built for NZ, with:
1. **Te Hiku Media partnership** for te reo TTS and Māori-language UI.
2. **Kaitiakitanga-style license** for iwi storytellers — explicit IP and royalty back to hapū.
3. Creator marketplace: retired DOC rangers, ex-teachers, novelists, kaumātua, iwi-appointed narrators.
4. **AI-personalised tours** — generates customised audio from a route + interest profile (geology, conservation, Māori history, food, wine), mixing pre-recorded narrator audio with on-device TTS.
5. GPS-triggered with offline mode (no signal on the West Coast).
6. Driving + walking variants.

**Pricing:** Tours NZD 8-25 each; bundles NZD 60-120 (e.g. "Te Waipounamu Grand Tour" — 15 routes). Creator royalty 50-65% à la VoiceMap. iwi co-published tours: 30-50% to iwi, 30-40% to platform, balance to other creators.

**Buyers:**
- B2C tourists (Australian / US drivers).
- **B2B licensing**: i-SITEs (60+), RTOs (31), DOC (Great Walks), iwi tourism arms, cruise operators (P&O, Princess), camper-van companies (THL, JUCY, Wilderness Motorhomes).
- **White-label**: branded for AA Traveller, AA Smartfuel, or as the in-car audio guide for Maui/Britz rentals.

**Math:** 50,000 tour-bundle sales/year at NZD 50 avg net = NZD 1.25M revenue × 12 (platform share ~35%) = NZD 5M ARR achievable in year 3 if i-SITE distribution lands. Add B2B licensing layer NZD 500k-1M.

**Why you win:** The Kaitiakitanga License is uncopyable by overseas operators. Te Hiku partnership locks in the te reo moat. NZ's road-trip market is genuinely huge per capita.

**Risks:** iwi engagement is slow (12-24 months for proper co-design). Mitigation: start with 2-3 iwi pilot regions (Ngāi Tahu, Ngāti Whātua, Tūhoe?) and expand by invitation.

### Play 3 — AI carbon ingestion for Toitū certification

**Problem:** Toitū-certified tourism operators average 33% emissions reduction — but the data ingest is manual. An operator burns 40-100 admin hours/year matching Genesis/Mercury bills, fuel cards, freight invoices and RMS Cloud occupancy into a GHG profile.

**Product:** An AI co-pilot that:
1. OCRs + classifies all energy/fuel/waste invoices automatically.
2. Pulls Genesis/Mercury/Contact/Meridian smart-meter data via APIs.
3. Pulls Z/BP fuel-card data via direct integrations.
4. Pulls occupancy from RMS Cloud, Seekom, Mews, SiteMinder.
5. Outputs a Toitū-ready carbon profile + EECA-aligned action recommendations.
6. Auto-generates Glasgow Declaration / XRB Climate-related Disclosure reports.

**Pricing:** NZD 300/mo small operator, NZD 800/mo mid (RMS Cloud-using), NZD 1,500/mo for 50+ room properties chasing CRD compliance. Toitū audit fee stays separate.

**Buyers:** Sudima (already on this path), Heritage Hotels, Distinction Hotels, larger TOP 10 parks, every Glasgow Declaration signatory in NZ tourism. ~500-1,000 operators in serious-buyer territory.

**Math:** 400 operators × NZD 600/mo avg = NZD 2.9M ARR by year 3.

**Why you win:** Toitū is a partnership channel — they want this and don't want to build software. EECA's Akiaki partnership is recently launched. CRD regime is hitting larger operators in 2025-26.

**Risks:** Weeva (Travalyst-owned) is the global threat — but their NZ presence is zero and they don't auto-pull NZ utility APIs.

### Play 4 — RTO-tier destination analytics

**Problem:** Tourism NZ is data-sophisticated (GuideGeek + Minecraft + Microsoft analytics). The 31 RTOs are not. They get visitor counts from Stats NZ and credit-card data from Marketview but no real-time AI flow forecasting, sentiment, or social listening tuned for tourism.

**Product:** A stripped-down Mabrian for RTOs:
1. Real-time sentiment + social listening across the destination.
2. Flight-demand feeds (where it's bookable, ex-NZ markets).
3. TECT/Marketview integration for credit-card spend.
4. Telco/mobility data for visitor flow (partnership with Vodafone/Spark).
5. Dispersal recommendations — "send these German campervan visitors to the Catlins next week."
6. Conversational AI dashboard (AILA-style) so RTO managers ask questions in English.

**Pricing:** NZD 25k/year (small RTO, Coromandel/West Coast/East Cape) up to NZD 60k/year (Destination Queenstown, Auckland Unlimited, ChristchurchNZ).

**Buyers:** All 31 RTOs + select bigger councils + maybe NZTE.

**Math:** 20 RTOs × NZD 40k avg = NZD 800k ARR. Slow but very sticky (council procurement = 3-5 year contracts).

**Why you win:** Mabrian / ADARA won't build at this price point. Tourism NZ's procurement preference is local. RTNZ membership is a single sales channel.

**Risks:** Government procurement cycles are slow; IVL spending freeze (per Newsroom Dec 2025) hits the supply-side budget pool. Mitigation: target council-funded RTOs (most are council-funded, not IVL-dependent).

### Play 5 — Mid-market motel/lodge AI concierge

**Problem:** Canary ($80M Series D, 20k properties), Duve ($60M Series B), HiJiffy and Asksuite all have weak direct sales in NZ. Yonder HQ (acquired by US TOMIS) and Myma.ai serve the top end. The 2,300 motels and holiday parks running RMS Cloud, Seekom or Booking.com messaging have nothing tuned for them.

**Product:** A vertical AI chatbot for the NZ-and-AU mid-market with:
1. Native integration with **RMS Cloud** (the dominant Aussie-built PMS in NZ) and **Seekom**, not just Mews/Cloudbeds.
2. **Mandarin + Korean + Japanese + Hindi + German** preset language stacks tuned for NZ-specific phrases (DOC hut, freedom camping rules, ferry timetables, weather alerts).
3. NZ-specific dispatch logic: airport transfers, ferry coordination (Bluebridge/Interislander), DOC hut overflow advice.
4. Booking.com / Expedia review-reply automation tuned for NZ tone.

**Pricing:** NZD 200/mo small motel, NZD 400/mo mid, NZD 600/mo holiday-park with multiple sites. Cheaper than Canary/Duve global tier; comparable to HiJiffy Pro.

**Buyers:** 2,300 motels and holiday parks; ~600 boutique lodges; TOP 10 Holiday Parks could be a group-buy.

**Math:** 600 properties × NZD 300/mo = NZD 2.2M ARR.

**Why you win:** RMS Cloud + Seekom integrations are the differentiator no global player will prioritise. TOP 10 group-buy unlocks 49 sites in one deal.

**Risks:** Yonder HQ + Myma.ai may move down-market. Mitigation: be faster, cheaper, and properly RMS Cloud-native.

## 5. Quick "do not do" list

- Don't build another **AI itinerary planner** (Booking.com, GuideGeek, Layla, Mindtrip already serve NZ travellers).
- Don't build **generic hotel revenue management** (RoomPriceGenie/Atomize own the indie segment; Duetto/IDeaS own enterprise).
- Don't build **generic rostering** (Deputy and Tanda already won NZ hospitality).
- Don't build **fraud / chargeback AI** (NZ tourism volumes too small).
- Don't ship **AI cultural storytelling** without explicit iwi co-design — reputational risk is existential.
- Don't pitch **OpenAI-only solutions to TNZ or Air NZ** — both are already deep with OpenAI directly.
