# A. Strategic narrative — what BC is trying to achieve

## A.1 Engineering workstream (22 live cards across 6 sub-clusters)

Bargain Chemist is a New Zealand pharmacy chain expanding its e-commerce operations. The Engineering workstream sits in a stabilise-and-handover phase: fixing operational pain in the order-to-fulfilment pipeline, modernising on-site search (post-Klevu), tightening Core Web Vitals + SEO, preparing the Australian market launch, and producing formal handover documentation that the previous agency (Zyber) is delivering to the new agency (Atlas Digital).

### A1 — Order & Fulfilment Ops (8 live, 2 done)
The single biggest active workload. Three threads run in parallel:
- **DoorDash same-day integration** (BC-2323, 25 comments — *heaviest thread in the whole corpus*) lets the BC portal adjust quantities or substitute items on active DoorDash orders. Backend controller + portal UI + delivery confirmation flow.
- **Order routing & lifecycle docs** (BC-2923) — formalising the Westgate / South Island Dark Store routing logic; both backend-friendly and client-friendly versions required. Order-editing flow QA on staging (BC-2328). One-off incidents like order #492992 bypassing the 2-unit stock buffer (BC-3078).
- **Fulfilment SLAs** (BC-2850) — bimodal fulfilment time (14h median S.I., 22h median Westgate), driving the unfulfilled-orders reporting work.
- **Escalations from MS Teams**: Hard Checkout Error (BC-2995, High) — cart/checkout extensions temporarily disabled while Toniq sync stabilises.
- **Australian launch** (BC-2793, on-hold) — Shopify Payments restricts pharmacy industry; alt payment-gateway scope blocked.
- **Shipping issue subtask** (BC-2792, on-hold) — free-delivery option not appearing under specific cart conditions.

### A2 — Inventory & Stock Sync (4 live, 3 done)
The single biggest *source* of operational pain. Toniq (POS, source-of-truth) drifts out of sync with Shopify, especially on new-product creation, with no visibility/reporting when mismatches occur (BC-2846). Multi-location inventory sync by store (BC-3003, 21 comments) and a custom Klaviyo trigger for interstore orders. Dark store stock incorrectly pulling from all locations (BC-3100). Per-location stock updates via custom integration (BC-1501, 25 comments).

### A3 — Search & Discovery (3 live, 2 done)
Post-Klevu modernisation. **AI Product FAQs pipeline** (BC-2925): GSC + GA4 internal search demand → human approval gate → SEO-friendly server-rendered publish to Shopify product pages. **Boost search GA4 tracking** (BC-2931): global GTM event for clicks on Boost search results, currently missing — blocks search-success reporting. **Filter tree audit** (BC-3034): Essano collection missing "Hand Wash" filter.

### A4 — Performance & SEO (3 live, 1 done)
External SEO consultancy (OMD) audit responses. **Core Web Vitals scope** (BC-2997) on the legacy theme. **Catastrophic metafield gap** (BC-2654): only 0.44% of products have meta titles; 17.94% have meta descriptions — proposed fix is a Shopify Flow auto-generating meta from existing description metafield. **Structured data on PLPs** (BC-3066) is generic/incomplete, blocking rich-result eligibility.

### A5 — Promos & Theme (2 live, 3 done)
April Sale theme duplication (BC-3042). Custom Klaviyo event for interstore-tagged orders to email customers about delivery delays (BC-2565, 12 comments — straddles A1/A5).

### A6 — Documentation (2 live, 2 done)
Formal handover documentation. **Backend** (BC-3076, **High**): portal architecture, third-party services, build/functionality docs, transitioning from PDF-on-disk to ClickUp-managed. **Frontend** (BC-3075): same scope on FE — third-party services, integrations, components.
